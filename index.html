METHODOLOGY

Every number shows its work.

Public venture figures often conflict. Verata shows the evidence behind each field, preserves uncertainty and leaves unsupported claims blank.

Sourced
Every field points back to public evidence such as filings, credible press and company disclosures.
Dated
Sources carry a publication or last-checked date so users can judge how current the claim is.
Graded
Confidence is visible. When the public record is incomplete, the product says so instead of guessing.

TRUST AND CONTROL

The organizer owns the room. Verata verifies the entities.

Clear boundaries protect the community while keeping public company information useful and checkable.

ROSTER CONTROL
Participation stays private

The organizer controls the participation roster and who can access its directory. Participation is never exposed across events or communities.

PUBLIC EVIDENCE
Entity profiles stay checkable

Verata maintains verified company and investor profiles from public sources. Each field carries its source, date and confidence.

MUTUAL INTENT
Discovery without contact dumps

The product is designed around intentional introductions. Sensitive contact information is not scraped, sold or exposed as a bulk list.

What Verata does not do: registration, ticketing, check-in, attendance tracking, contact dumps, risk ratings or fundraising guarantees.
